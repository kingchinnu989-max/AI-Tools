Our Philosophy

Recipes born in grandmothers' kitchens.

Founded in 2011, Rangoli has been Hyderabad's most beloved destination for authentic Indian cuisine. Our head chef, trained in Lucknow and Jaipur, brings royal recipes to your table using hand-ground masalas, fresh local produce, and slow-cooking methods passed down through generations.

We believe food is culture. Every plate tells the story of a region, a season, and a family tradition.
