The Eye
Behind

I am a visual storyteller drawn to the poetry hidden inside ordinary moments — the way afternoon light fractures through glass, how shadows define architecture, the weight of silence in a crowded street.

Every photograph is a negotiation between what I see and what I feel. My work spans editorial portraiture, documentary street photography, and cinematic landscape — united by an obsession with mood, texture, and time.

Based in New Delhi. Available worldwide for editorial, commercial, and personal projects.
